## THE LEGEND OF THE NINE ISLES

Every sea is born from eight hex digits. Share a seed, share a world — but
no two captains need ever sail the same ocean.

You are a pirate captain with a small ship, a blank chart, and an ocean
that has never existed before. Somewhere out there, ringing the center of
the world, lie the **Nine Isles of Legend**. Each isle is guarded by a
jealous sentinel ship, and each hides a fragment of an ancient chart.

Assemble all nine fragments, survive the final fleet that rises to defend
the secret, and the **Treasure of the Nine Isles** is yours.

Fair winds, Captain. The sea is waiting.

---

## CONTROLLER OPERATION

**+ Control Pad**

*At sea:* Trim the sails. Your ship has momentum — she will coast, drift,
and carry her way, so ease off before the shore!
*In port and on menus:* Move the cursor.
*On the seed screen:* LEFT and RIGHT choose a digit; UP and DOWN change it.

**A Button**

*At sea:* Dock at a friendly beach, fire the cannons, or dig for treasure
on an Isle of Legend.
*In port:* Confirm a selection.
*On the title screen:* Begin.

**B Button**

*In port:* Back out of a menu, or set sail from the main menu.
*At sea:* Weigh anchor and return to the seed screen. Mind you record
the voyage in port first — leaving the helm writes nothing to the Log.

**START Button**

*At sea:* Open the Sea Chart.
*On the title screen:* Begin.
*On the seed screen:* Continue a saved voyage.

## BEGINNING YOUR VOYAGE

Insert the Game Pak and turn the power switch to ON. After the title
screen, press START or A to reach the **Seed Screen**.

Eight hex digits make an ocean. Use LEFT and RIGHT to choose a digit and
UP and DOWN to change it, then press **A** to set sail on a brand-new sea.
Every captain's first sea ought to be `DEADBEEF` — a fine, lucky number.

If a voyage is already recorded in the Captain's Log, its seed appears on
the screen. Press **START** to continue that voyage where you left off.

There are 65,536 seas awaiting. No two alike.

## THE SHIP'S HUD

At the bottom of the screen, your instruments keep two rows of readings:

* **TX** — your east-west position, in hex, by tile.
* **TY** — your north-south position, in hex, by tile.
* **SPD** — your current speed. Watch it near land!
* **H** — hull remaining. When it reaches zero, see SHIPWRECK.
* **G** — gold in the hold.
* **F** — chart fragments found, out of 9.

A good navigator notes the coordinates of anything worth finding again.

## SAILING THE OPEN SEA

The action moves in every direction, Captain — the whole world is yours
the moment you leave the dock.

* Hold a direction to build speed; release it and the ship coasts to a
  stop. She answers slowly, like any proud vessel.
* **Ramming land damages the hull.** Come about before the sandbar, not
  after.
* Sailing into a new part of the world inks it onto your Sea Chart.
  Newly charted waters are not always empty — pirates and storms favor
  the unknown.
* Shallow, speckled water means land is near. Watch for beaches with
  wooden **dock planks** — those are ports, and friends.

---

## THE SEA CHART

Press **START** at sea to unroll your chart. Waters you have sailed are
inked in — sea and land — while the rest stays blank parchment.

* Your ship's position is marked on the chart.
* Ports you have docked at are marked with a little harbor house. You
  need only visit a port once to remember it forever.
* Press A, B, or START to return to the helm.

Chart everything. A blank chart makes a poor pirate and a rich undertaker.

## HAZARDS OF THE DEEP

**Pirates.** Rival scoundrels prowl newly charted waters — perhaps one
voyage in five. They will close with you and open fire. Press **A** to
answer with your own broadside; your gunners aim true at any enemy in
sight. Sink a pirate and plunder his gold.

**Storms.** Dark weather blows up without warning and drags the ship
where it will — hard enough that a bold captain can ride the blow like a
fast current, if he dares. Keep a firm hand on the helm: a captain who
fights the rudder keeps his hull; a captain who abandons it does not.

**Hull is life.** Enemy shot, hidden rocks, and storm-tossed drifting all
cost hull. When the hull reaches zero, see SHIPWRECK, and say a prayer.

## LIFE IN PORT

Beaches with dock planks are ports. Sail alongside and press **A** to
dock. Every port offers:

* **TRADE** — Buy low, sell high. Four goods move on every market: RUM,
  SILK, SPICE, and CANNON. Prices drift from port to port; the wise
  captain remembers where spice was cheap. RIGHT buys, LEFT sells, and
  the hold fits 50 barrels in all.
* **REPAIR** — The shipwright patches your hull, 2 gold per point.
* **TAVERN** — See below. Worth the price of admission alone.
* **RECRUIT** — Hire able seamen, 5 gold a head. A well-manned ship is a
  happy ship.
* **SAVE** — Record the voyage in the Captain's Log.
* **SET SAIL** — Back to the horizon.

The Captain's Log is also written automatically whenever you dock or set
sail. Thoughtful of the quartermaster, that.

---

## THE TAVERN

Every tavern keeps three kinds of treasure: gossip, directions, and more
gossip.

* A bit of local **flavor** — believe half of what you hear.
* The name, bearing, and distance of the **nearest other port** —
  measured in days' sailing.
* Word of the **nearest unclaimed Isle of Legend** — its legendary name,
  how many days distant, and the heading to steer.

When you're lost, out of gold, or out of ideas, the tavern knows.

---

## THE NINE ISLES OF LEGEND

Nine isles ring the center of the world, and every sea hides them in
different waters. Their names are spoken in every tavern:

    LIBERTALIA      WHYDAH DEEP     KRAKEN SKERRY
    THE LOCKER      OLD ROGER ROCK  KIDDS CACHE
    FIDDLERS GREEN  DUTCHMAN CAPE   MAROON SPIT

Rumor has it each isle is watched by a **guardian ship**, tougher and
quicker on the trigger than any common pirate. Sail into an isle's waters
and its guardian will find *you*.

---

## DIGGING FOR FRAGMENTS

Each isle's beach hides a **chart fragment** — but the sand only gives it
up once the isle's guardian lies at the bottom of the sea.

1. Find an isle (the tavern always knows the nearest).
2. Sink its guardian.
3. Land on its beach and press **A** to dig.

With every fragment the screen tells you how many you hold — *three of
nine* and counting. X marks the spot, Captain, nine times over.

---

## THE FINAL BATTLE

The ninth fragment is not the end. It is the beginning of the end.

Assembling the complete chart wakes the **final fleet** — guardian ships
rising one after another to defend the Treasure of the Nine Isles. Sink
them all. There is no retreat, no repair, no mercy.

Survive, and the Treasure — and your final haul of gold — is yours.
The screen will say the rest.

---

## SHIPWRECK

When the hull reaches zero, the sea takes her due: you lose **half your
gold**, and wake in open water with a patched hull and your pride in
tatters.

The voyage continues — a pirate does not die so easily — but Davy Jones
keeps a ledger, and he always collects.

---

## THE CAPTAIN'S LOG (SAVING)

Your voyage is recorded automatically:

* whenever you **dock** at a port,
* whenever you **set sail**,
* and on the day you **win the Treasure**.

To continue a recorded voyage, press **START** on the seed screen. The
Log keeps your seed, your gold, your cargo, your hull and crew, your
chart, your fragments, and every port you've ever called at. The isles
themselves need no remembering — the seed always knows where they lie.

---

## CAPTAIN'S SECRETS

* Fire the cannons with no enemy in sight and the shot flies straight
  ahead — useful for discouraging whatever is *about* to be in sight.
* Storms cannot hurt a captain who keeps steering. Idle hands are the
  real hazard.
* An isle's waters roll no random encounters. The guardian is hazard
  enough.
* Prices differ from port to port. Buy your cannon where they're cheap,
  sell them where they're dear, and let the merchants call it piracy.
* A wreck costs half your gold. Gold spent in port, on the other hand,
  cannot be lost at sea.

## PRECAUTIONS & WARRANTY

   hour or so. Even pirates rest.
2. Do not store the Game Pak in conditions of extreme temperature, and
   never, ever let it get wet. Salt water is death to a cartridge.
3. Keep this booklet in its protective case when not in use.
